6 · AFTER — what the logs now tell you
Every sweep records its window and what it refused to touch — the missing counter that made this bug hard to see.

The rule the app now follows on every call

Call ends (receiver)
→
Read ONLY the last 5 minutes of the log
→
Row older than 5 min?
→
ignored as history
└→
Row is live → create reminder
→
ONE alert for the whole sweep
App start
→
no scan (anchor only)
·
Open Calls tab
→
no scan
·
Manual scan button
→
same 5-minute window
Trade-off, stated plainly: a call whose log row the phone writes more than five minutes late is not picked up. That is the price of never reading history — the two automatic retries (+10 s, +60 s) cover the normal OEM delay.
